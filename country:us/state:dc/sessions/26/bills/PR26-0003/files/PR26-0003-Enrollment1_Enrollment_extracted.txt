ENROLLED ORIGINAL
A RESOLUTION
26-3
IN THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA
January 2, 2025
RESOLVED, BY THE COUNCIL OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA, That this
resolution may be cited as the “General Counsel to the Council of the District of Columbia
Nicole Streeter Reappointment Resolution of 2025”.
Sec. 2. The Council of the District of Columbia reappoints:
Ms. Nicole Streeter
12th Street, N.W.
Washington, D.C. 20009
(Ward 1)
as the General Counsel to the Council of the District of Columbia, beginning January 2, 2025.
Sec. 3. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
